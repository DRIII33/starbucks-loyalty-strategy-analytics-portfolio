# Project Disclaimer

Independent educational portfolio project; not commissioned, sponsored, approved or endorsed by Starbucks Coffee Company. All customer, transaction, store, product, offer, assignment, redemption and analysis records are synthetic. They do not describe actual Starbucks customers, sales, offers, systems, internal KPIs or financial results. The campaign and business scenario are hypothetical. This project cannot establish why Starbucks opened the historical job requisition.

Tier labels and campaign rules are simplified teaching constructs, not a faithful implementation of Starbucks Rewards. The 58% contribution-rate assumption is illustrative and not Starbucks-reported data. Outputs are not for production decisions or financial reporting. A real implementation would require authorized data, validated definitions, privacy review, Finance-approved economics, power analysis, governance and independent QA.
